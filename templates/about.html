Final Year Capstone Project

SmartRetail AI

Intelligent Business Analytics Dashboard with Sales Forecasting and Decision Support Using Machine Learning

A comprehensive, commercial-grade decision support platform engineered for retail enterprises. The application unites exploratory data analysis, interactive multi-dimensional business intelligence, time-series machine learning forecasting, and automated prescriptive strategy recommendations.

1. Descriptive & Diagnostic BI

Calculates dynamic KPIs across 9,994 transactional records. Evaluates 10 analytical dimensions covering seasonal trends, category margin variations, regional performance disparities, and discount profit cannibalization.

2. Predictive Sales Forecasting

Employs time-series regression algorithms (Linear Regression & Random Forest) trained on 2014–2016 monthly aggregates. Projects up to 12 months ahead (2018) while preventing data leakage through strict chronological evaluation.

3. Prescriptive Decision Support

Transforms raw data patterns into actionable executive directives: enforces the 20% discount ceiling, identifies persistent hardware loss leaders, capitalizes on Technology category strengths, and plans Q4 seasonal inventory.
